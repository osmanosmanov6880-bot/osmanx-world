THE OWNER’S CHALLENGE

What happens when the right size is unavailable?

What we test

Separate lack of demand from lack of availability or a convenient next step. Test reservations or stock discovery across locations.

An illustrative scenario. Causes are established using the individual business’s data.

Where to start

One category in one store. Record requests, test one reservation or notification journey and compare completed actions. Quoted after scoping.

Build a pilot brief

What you receive

  • An anonymised map of unmet requests
  • A prototype or integration for one journey
  • A clear workflow for sales staff
  • A review of results and staff workload

How we evaluate

Unavailable-size requests, confirmed reservations and completed purchases. A reservation alone is not a sale.

When to expand

Add a catalogue, app or multi-store connection when the need is demonstrated.
